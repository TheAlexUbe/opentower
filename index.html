RADIO LANGUAGE · IDIOMA

Radio language · Idioma de radio: switches ATC/pilot phraseology, speech recognition and quick commands. Los menús siguen en inglés.

No account. No paywall. Runs entirely in your browser.

Tip: pause and 1× / 2× / 4× speed live in the header. Speech recognition needs Chrome/Edge; typing always works.

1 Click a strip or aircraft.
2 Type your clearance: N123AB taxi to runway 27.
3 Then: line up and wait, cleared for takeoff, cleared to land, taxi to the ramp.
4 North ramp: cross runway 12 at the hold.
5 Approach mode: turn left heading 270, descend 3000, cleared ILS, contact tower.
6 Español: N123AB ruede a la pista 27 por alfa, autorizado para despegar, autorizado para aterrizar.
7 Voice: click MIC (or press M) and speak. Chrome/Edge only; Brave may block speech recognition. V mutes pilot voices.
